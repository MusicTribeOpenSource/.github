# Names and logos

Open source means you can use, change and share our code and designs. It doesn't mean our names and
logos come with them. This page explains the difference, so you can build freely without stepping on
anything.

Music Tribe, Behringer and the other Music Tribe brand names and logos are trademarks of their owners
in the Music Tribe group. Names of other companies' products belong to those companies.

## You can

- **Say what your work is compatible with.** "Firmware for the Behringer XYZ", "works with Engine OS".
- **Say where your work came from.** "Based on Music Tribe Open Source."
- **Talk about us** in articles, videos, reviews and forums.

## Please don't

- **Brand your project or product with our names or logos.** If you build and sell hardware from our
  designs, give it your own name. That's what keeps buyers from confusing your product with ours.
- **Suggest we made, approve or support something we didn't.**
- **Use other companies' names or logos** to brand your work here.

## Not sure?

Ask on [Discord](https://discord.gg/gS6qtuShvw) before you publish. We'd much rather help you get it
right than ask you to change it later.
