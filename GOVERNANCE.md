# How we work together

This page explains who does what here, and how decisions get made.

## Contributors: everyone

Anyone can contribute. You don't join anything, sign anything or pay anything: fork a repository and
open a pull request. Contributors aren't members of this organization, and they never need a company
account.

## Maintainers: trusted community members

Maintainers review and merge pull requests for the repositories they look after. They are community
members who have shown good judgement over time: helpful reviews, solid contributions, kindness to
newcomers.

- **How you become one:** an existing maintainer nominates you, and the owners confirm.
- **What you get:** review and merge rights on specific public repositories.
- **What you give:** timely, friendly reviews, and care for the rules in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- **Stepping back is normal.** Life changes. Tell us and we'll thank you and hand over.

## Owners: the organization's stewards

A small group from the Foundation looks after the organization itself: settings, security, legal
questions, the brands, and confirming new maintainers. Owners don't control what the community builds.

## How decisions are made

- **Everyday changes** are decided in the pull request. If the maintainers agree and nobody raises a
  reasonable objection, it goes in.
- **Bigger directions** (a new repository, a major redesign) are discussed openly in an issue or on
  Discord before any work starts.
- **What hardware we build next** is decided by community vote.
- **Legal, safety and brand questions** are the one place the owners have the final say, because they
  carry the responsibility if something goes wrong.

## Changing this page

Propose a change with a pull request, like anything else.
